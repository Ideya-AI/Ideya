About

A visual wiki for hard issues.

Infinity is a curated atlas of humanity's hardest issues — existential risk, governance, economics, ethics. It sits between an encyclopedia and a debate platform: enough structure to understand an issue quickly, enough honesty to show where the real disagreement sits.

01

One spine, six sections

Every topic is one structured infographic, always in the same order: Overview, Basics, Questions, Positions, Crux, Actions. Orientation first, numbers next, then the debate — and finally what to do about it.

02

Read at your depth

The same page works as a glanceable poster, a normal read, or a deep dive with sources and curated next steps. Depth expands within sections — it never swaps the page out from under you.

03

Curation leads, participation refines

Editors map each issue; readers vote on what matters and submit their own view. Votes reorder questions and views are aggregated into the landscape — nothing here is a feed, and nothing is a comment section.

How it's written
  • Neutral by intention. No house position. The ideas take the lead — there is no bias-balance theater, just an honest map.
  • Every position is a steelman. Each side is written in its own strongest voice, the way its smartest proponent would put it.
  • The crux is the point. Each topic narrows to where the disagreement actually sits — usually a smaller, sharper question than the headline fight.
  • Facts before debate. The shared empirical landscape comes first, with sources, so disagreement starts from common ground.
