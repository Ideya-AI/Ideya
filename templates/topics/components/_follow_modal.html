{% comment %}Vote follow-up modal — opens when a reader upvotes a question or a position, leans to one of a question's poles, or taps the respond button under an expanded fact / question / position. The shell is static; the response fills #follow-eyebrow and #follow-body out of band (_follow_body.html via hx-swap-oob) and fires the "follow-up" event that opens it. Nothing is required; closing (X / Done / scrim / Esc) just dismisses — saved answers persist.{% endcomment %}
