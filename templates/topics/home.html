The project

Make the shape of hard issues legible.

Ideya maps humanity’s most important issues — existential risk, governance, economics, ethics. Each topic lays out what’s known, the open questions, the main views, and where they split — so you can find where you stand, and why.

01

What it is

A visual wiki between an encyclopedia and a debate platform. Every topic is one structured page on the same six-section spine — overview, basics, questions, positions, crux, actions. Learn it once, navigate them all.

02

Why it exists

Most coverage flattens hard issues into verdicts or drowns them in takes. Ideya takes no side: every position gets its strongest case, in its own voice — you can't honestly disagree with an argument you've only met at its weakest.

03

How it works

Read at the depth you have time for — glance at the shape of a debate, or go deep into sourced numbers and curated next steps. Editors keep the map accurate; your votes surface what matters. Curation leads, participation refines.
