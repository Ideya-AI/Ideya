The project

Make the shape of hard issues legible.

Infinity is a curated atlas of humanity’s hardest issues — existential risk, governance, economics, ethics. For each one: what it actually is, where the live disagreements sit, who holds which position and why, and how to engage. Topic-first. Neutral by intention.

01

What it is

A visual wiki between an encyclopedia and a debate platform. Every topic is one structured infographic — six sections, always in the same order.

02

Why it exists

Most sources either flatten disagreement or drown in it. Infinity surfaces the real contest — fairly, without a house position — so you can think, not just take a side.

03

How it works

Read at the depth you want — a glanceable poster, a full page, or deep with curated sources. Vote to surface what matters. Curation leads; participation refines.
