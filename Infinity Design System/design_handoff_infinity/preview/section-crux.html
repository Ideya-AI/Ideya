Content

  • 1–3 paragraphs of original synthesis — the most editorial section.
  • Names where the disagreement narrows, tighter than the headlines.
  • Usually a values-vs-empirical split or one decisive question.
  • The “why not Wikipedia” payload — it must say something genuinely new.

Design

  • Dark inverted band — the single tonal flip on the page, set apart.
  • Lead line in Jakarta 800; one green highlight for the pivot phrase.
  • References positions by their colors when it cites a side.
  • No vote/agree controls — the Crux is read, not rated.

Interaction & expansion

  • Read-only. The one section with no participation affordance.
  • Depth grows prose: 1 line → paragraph → full + “go further.”
  • “Go further” opens curated sources for the fault line itself.
  • Always present when shared — the lead line is the share quote.
