Questions

Surface the contested questions cleanly — and signal user agency without becoming a forum. 4–6 open questions that drive the disagreement. They set up the Positions that follow and the Crux that resolves them.

Every question is typed the section's core distinction

Empiricalresolvable with data

A question where better evidence could move the answer — costs, death rates, deployment speed. The reader could imagine a study that settles it.

Valuerequires judgment

A question where no amount of data decides it — how to weigh dread risk, who bears local harm, what we owe the future. It needs a choice.

Content

  • 4–6 open questions that genuinely drive the disagreement.
  • Each: question + 1–2 sentence “why it's contested.”
  • A mix of empirical and value — label every one.
  • Phrased neutrally; never leading toward an answer.

Design

  • Question in Jakarta 800; “why” in plain ink body.
  • Empirical = blue, Value = amber tag — same hues as the Crux split.
  • Vote control on the left rail; tabular count.
  • Poster: numbered list, no votes. Web/mobile: interactive rows.

Interaction & expansion

  • Importance up/down → gradually reorders the list (unique to Questions).
  • Not a forum: no replies, no threads — only importance.
  • Depth grows: question → +why → +links to positions.
  • Condensed/print freezes a sensible default order; no live votes.
