Anatomy of a position the full (deep) card

Climate Pragmatist

“The only proven, scaled, firm low-carbon source we have.”

Nuclear is the only dispatchable zero-carbon power that already runs at scale. IPCC pathways to 1.5 °C lean on expanding it; Germany's reactor phase-out raised emissions. Firmness, not just cleanliness, is the point.

Representative: James Hansen — climate scientist who broke with much of the green movement to back nuclear.

Proponents: James Hansen · Stewart Brand · Clean Air Task Force

Content

  • 3–5 genuine positions — the real camps, not left/right by default.
  • Each: name · steelman · description · representative · proponents.
  • Positions are non-1:1 with Questions — one can span several.
  • Written in the side's own voice; the product holds no house view.

Design

  • Color rail + dot + Jakarta-800 name is the fixed identity.
  • Equal-weight cards; no side larger or first-by-rank visually.
  • Steelman is italic; description is plain ink body.
  • Poster: 2×2 grid of cards. Web: 2-col. Mobile: stacked accordions.

Interaction & expansion

  • Agree / disagree tints with the position color when active.
  • Importance vote up/down — aggregates, never reorders the sides.
  • Tap a card → description + representative + “go further” expand.
  • Static & complete when shared: legend + names + steelman always render.
