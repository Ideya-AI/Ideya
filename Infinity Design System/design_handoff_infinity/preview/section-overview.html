Content

  • Title + icon · 1–2 sentence description.

Design

  • Icon + Jakarta-800 title = the same lockup the share card uses.
  • Category badges in green; related chips are neutral outline.
  • Scope/Why-now as two small cards, not prose paragraphs.
  • Poster: title + description hero. Mobile: badges wrap above title.
