# Category primary icon art — briefs for all 9 categories

Handoff briefs for Nano Banana / Gemini. Full locked style: `../../ICONOGRAPHY.md`
and the worked example `nuclear-energy.md`. Read those once; every prompt below
assumes that style.

## What a category icon is
The **category page hero** (header tile + OG card). Broader than a topic icon: it
should evoke the *whole domain*, not one issue. Same discipline as topic art —
must read large and small, on light and dark. Save each as
`static/img/categories/<slug>.png` (transparent or white ground).

## Style (identical to topics — baked into every prompt below)
Minimal **monoline**, ONE uniform stroke weight, rounded caps/joins. **Duotone,
single hue, two tones** — solid line + a clearly visible ~15–18% fill of the same
hue on the main masses (Phosphor-duotone logic). **Frameless** (the CSS tile draws
the frame). A small balanced **scene** of a few simple sub-icons — richness is the
combination, not detail on any one element. Plain white background, no text,
neutral/editorial mood. Each category uses its **own colour** (hex below).

Neutrality note: like the topics, these domains scramble partisan lines — the art
should feel even-handed (promise *and* tension), never advocacy.

Per category: **over-generate the element flagged ⚠︎** — it's the one most likely
to render wrong. The edit-mode path (feed the first result back in and request
tweaks) usually beats re-rolling; see `nuclear-energy.md`.

## AI & Machine Intelligence — `ai` — tech blue `#3b7ea1`
Elements: a processor chip with a few circuit traces (compute · focal), a small neural-network node graph (connected dots ⚠︎), a robot/machine head in profile, a human head silhouette facing it (the human–machine relationship).

**Prompt:**
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon of an artificial-intelligence scene, clean editorial line-art. Combine a few simple symbols into one small, balanced composition: a computer processor chip with short circuit traces as the focal point, a small neural-network graph of connected dots, a simple robot head in profile, and a human head silhouette facing it. NO border, NO frame, NO box around the scene — just the illustration on a plain white background. Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element (the circuit traces and node links must be exactly as thick as the outlines — do not let them render thinner), rounded caps and corners, confident slightly bold strokes, legible when small, no fussy detail. Colour: a single blue hue, two tones only — draw all lines in solid blue #3b7ea1 and give the main masses (the chip, the two heads) a clearly visible soft fill of the same blue at ~15–18% opacity so the duotone reads. No other colours, gradients, shadows, 3D, or texture. Composition: centred, generous even padding, balanced optical weight. Calm, neutral, editorial mood — even-handed about AI. No text or letters.
