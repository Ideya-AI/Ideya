# Vaccine Mandates & Public Health Authority — primary icon art brief

Handoff brief for an illustrator or image/vector tool. Full style theme: `../ICONOGRAPHY.md`.
Template siblings: `nuclear-energy.md` (the tuned reference), `ai-alignment.md` and
`frontier-ai-regulation.md` (most recent), `birth-rate-decline-pronatalism.md`.
First topic in **Bio, Health & the Human Body** — sets the rose reference.

## Use
The topic's **primary hero image**. Shows large on the topic page, poster, and social
cards; also reduced into a rounded-square tinted tile and shown on a dark band. Must
read both large and small, on light and dark.

## Style (non-negotiable)
- **Minimal monoline** — ONE consistent stroke weight across the whole image, rounded
  caps/joins. References: **Gorges Beer Co** menu icons, **Flaticon "Lineal."**
- **Duotone, single color, two tones** — line at full strength + a soft (~16–18%) fill
  of the **same hue** on the masses. No second color, no gradients, no shadows.
- **Color:** the category color (Bio, Health & the Human Body = **bio rose**,
  `#b0567f`). Deliver single-color so it can be recolored per category.
- Flat, clean, editorial.
- **Filled masses, not linework-only** — lesson from earlier topics: thin unfilled
  linework nearly vanished at 40px. **Every** major element in this scene is a filled
  shape carrying the duotone tint; nothing important may be a bare line.

## Composition
A small bespoke **scene** combining several *simple* sub-icons in one frame — about
**2.5–3.5× the complexity of a single Gorges icon**. Generate wide (3:2); framing is
normalized in post (trim to content, ~8% pad, fit 3:2 on white). Legible from ~40px
to hero size, readable on a dark background.

An **object-only still life** on one ground line — no human figure, no hand, no arm, no
child, no crowd. Three objects: the place where the requirement bites, the paper that
decides who is exempt from it, and the instrument itself. Its silhouette is a **tall
peaked mass beside two low flat ones**, which is what separates it at tile size from
`frontier-ai-regulation`'s all-low still life; where that scene's page is the rule not
yet written, this one's page is the exemption not yet claimed.

The subject is **consent meeting requirement**, not a resolution of the two: a school
whose door is neither barred nor thrown open, a form nobody has signed, and a syringe
that has not been used. Nothing in the frame says which way any of it goes.

## Elements — promise *and* peril
The debate is how far the state may go in requiring vaccination for school or work —
the reach of *Jacobson*-era police power, which diseases qualify, how wide the
exemptions run, who enforces, and what enforcement does to trust. Seven camps run from
herd-immunity floors through institutional risk-bearing and schedule minimalism to
medical-freedom objection and confidence-building. The art must hold the *question*
open — it must not celebrate a mandate and must not warn against one:
- **A plain schoolhouse standing on the ground line** — the focal mass on one side:
  the place the requirement actually bites, since school entry is where nearly every
  mandate lands. A simple rectangular building with a triangular gabled roof, one plain
  door centered in the front wall and one small square window. The door is **simply
  drawn in the wall — neither swung open nor blocked**: no bars, no lock, no chain, no
  gate, no fence, no rope, no barrier, no closed sign, and nobody standing at it. An
  open door admits everyone (verdict: the mandate is unnecessary); a barred one turns
  the child away (verdict: the mandate excludes). Filled mass.
- **A single blank form lying flat on the ground in the open middle, with one empty
  signature line** — the exemption, which is the actual battleground: how easy the form
  is to get is what decides coverage, far more than the mandate's wording. A plain
  rectangular sheet, **completely blank except for ONE short horizontal rule near its
  lower edge** — an empty place to sign. **Nothing is written on it**: no words, no
  paragraphs of text lines, no checkboxes, no ticks, no crosses, no stamp, no seal, and
  no signature on the rule. Unsigned is the whole point — the claim has not been made
  and has not been refused. It lies alone in the middle and **touches neither** the
  school nor the syringe. Filled mass.
- **A syringe lying flat and at rest on the same ground line ⚠︎** — the counterweight
  on the other side, echoing the topic's Phosphor `syringe` glyph: the requirement's
  object, drawn as an instrument set down rather than an act performed. Seen side-on: a
  plain barrel with a plunger at one end and a short straight needle at the other,
  **level, still, empty, and undischarged** — no droplet, no spray, no jet, no motion
  lines, no vial or bottle, and **no arm, no skin, and nothing being injected**. The
  needle lies parallel to the ground and **points at neither the school nor the paper**;
  a needle aimed at anything reads as a threat, and a needle in an arm reads as the
  question already settled. Never crossed out or struck through. Filled mass.
- **A single straight horizontal ground line across the scene** — one plane under the
  schoolhouse, the form, and the syringe alike (mirrors the sibling compositions): the
  institution, the exemption, and the shot stand on the same ground, none above another.

The neutrality is structural: three objects of comparable weight on one level line, a
door that is neither open nor barred, an unsigned page between them, and no arrow,
tether, bracket, enclosure, or connecting line joining any of them.

## Mood
Neutral and editorial — a school standing open-question, a form waiting for a name, an
instrument set down. Whether the requirement is legitimate, how wide the exemption
should run, and what enforcement costs in trust are the questions the scene holds. Not
a public-health triumph, not a coercion warning.

## Avoid
Photorealism; more than one color; gradients/shadows; **shields**, protective domes,
bubbles, umbrellas, halos, or anything sheltering the school or a figure (verdict:
mandates are protection, so mandates are right); checkmarks, ticks, green-light marks,
a stamped or signed form, a completed checklist, laurels, medals, or a heart
(verdict: compliance achieved); **a crossed-out, struck-through, forbidden, or
circle-and-slash syringe**, a broken or bent needle, a syringe tipped over, snapped, or
in a trash bin (verdict: anti-mandate); a needle entering an arm, piercing skin, a
recoiling body, a restrained or held-down figure, a bandage, a tear, or a blood drop
(verdict: coercion); any authority symbol looming over people or over the school — a
gavel, a badge, a uniform, an official cap, a government seal, a pointing hand, a
giant hand, a courthouse or capitol dome, a flag or flagpole, a scroll of law
(verdict: the state overrides the person); bars, cages, fences, gates, padlocks,
chains, ropes, barriers, or a "closed" sign on the schoolhouse door (verdict: the
unvaccinated are shut out), and equally an open door with a welcoming figure or a
stream of children entering (verdict: the mandate is unneeded); viruses, germs, spike
proteins, contagion clouds, warning triangles, hazard stripes, biohazard marks, red
zones, or skulls (verdict: the threat justifies the mandate); rising or falling line
charts, coverage dials, gauges, percentage rings, thermometers, progress bars, or
countdown clocks (verdict: the numbers decide it); any human figure, hand, arm, face,
or silhouette at all; balance scales, blank rule-books, gavels, or pens (those belong
to the governance and AI-regulation scenes and must not be reused here); fussy internal
detail (bricks, roof tiles, window panes, or a door handle on the school; ruled lines,
margins, or a watermark on the form; measurement gradations or numbers on the syringe
barrel); clutter; anything that reads as pro- or anti-mandate.

## Deliverable
Ideally **vector (SVG)**, single-color, transparent ground. PNG accepted
(the pipeline is raster-primary).

## Generation prompt (Nano Banana / Gemini)
**Frameless** — the art has NO drawn border. The CSS tile renders the rounded-square
frame. Lessons carried over from the earlier generations: force the soft fill to
~15–18% (it renders near-invisible if unstressed), force uniform stroke weight (thin
secondary elements drift thinner), and make every major element a **filled mass** —
earlier linework-only elements nearly vanished at 40px.

**Edit-mode instruction (after the first generation, feed the concept back in):**
> Using this image, keep the same composition, elements, and layout, but: (1) remove any border or frame around the whole scene entirely; (2) add a clearly visible soft light-rose fill (~16% opacity of the same hue) inside EVERY major element — the schoolhouse body and roof, the sheet of paper, and the syringe's barrel and plunger — so it reads as a two-tone duotone with no outline-only elements left; (3) make every line exactly the same thickness, matching the main outline weight — including the school's door and window, the signature rule on the paper, the syringe's needle, and the ground line; (4) lay the syringe completely flat, level and at rest on the ground line with its needle parallel to the ground and pointing at neither the school nor the paper, and delete any droplet, spray, liquid, motion line, vial, arm, skin, or crossing-out over it; (5) keep the sheet of paper blank except for one short empty horizontal signature rule near its lower edge — remove any writing, text lines, checkboxes, ticks, crosses, stamp, seal or signature; (6) keep the schoolhouse plain with a door simply drawn in the wall — remove any bars, lock, chain, gate, fence, rope, barrier or sign on the door, and remove any flag, flagpole or bell tower; (7) keep the schoolhouse, the sheet and the syringe at equal visual weight and equal height on the same ground line, none above the others, with a clear gap so the sheet touches neither; (8) remove any shield, dome, warning triangle, virus, checkmark, badge, gavel or human figure. Keep it flat, single rose hue, no gradients, shadows, extra colors, or text.

**Notes:**
- Over-generate on the **syringe at rest ⚠︎** — it is by far the element most likely to
  come out wrong, and it can fail in both directions. The generator wants to stand it
  upright with a droplet at the needle tip, tilt it into an injecting pose, drive it
  into an arm, or add a spray or motion arc — all of which render the verdict "the shot
  is being administered." Given any hint of tension it flips to the mirror failure: a
  slash through the syringe, a red circle-and-slash, a bent or snapped needle, a
  toppled syringe. Either one answers the question the scene exists to hold open.
- Over-generate on the **unsigned form** too. The generator badly wants to fill the
  sheet with lines of text, add a heading, a seal, a row of checkboxes with one ticked,
  or a signature scrawled on the rule. A ticked box or a signature says the exemption
  was granted; a stamp says it was processed. The page must read as not yet acted on.
- Watch for the **schoolhouse door being editorialized** — bars, a padlock, a chain, a
  gate, or a closed sign across it reads "the unvaccinated are shut out," while a wide
  open door with children streaming in reads "no requirement needed." Neither belongs;
  the door is a plain shape in the wall.
- Watch for authority symbols entering the frame — a gavel, a badge, a uniform, an
  official seal, a flag on the school, a capitol dome, or a giant pointing hand over
  the building. The scene names no authority; the empty signature line is the only
  statement about who decides.
- Watch for the threat being drawn in — a virus, germ, spike ball, contagion cloud,
  biohazard mark, or warning triangle. Drawing the pathogen argues the mandate's case
  and disqualifies the candidate; so does a shield, dome, or halo over the school.
- Watch for any human figure, hand, or face appearing anywhere — this scene has no
  people in it at all, which is also what keeps it from taking a side about whose body
  is at stake.
- Watch for outline-only elements — every major mass must carry the rose fill; a
  candidate whose sheet, syringe barrel, or school walls render as bare linework fails
  the 40px test.
- White background is intentional — the primary sits on a neutral tile; the frame
  comes from the tile, not the art.
- After generating: save as `static/img/topics/vaccine-mandates.png`,
  set `Topic.primary_art = "img/topics/vaccine-mandates.png"`, and
  regenerate the social PNGs
  (`render_social --slug vaccine-mandates --formats og,sq,pt`).
