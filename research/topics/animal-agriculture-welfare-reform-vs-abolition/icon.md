## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a large standing pig in side view as the dominant focal mass in the foreground, calm and plain; behind it a long low livestock barn with a row of roof vents and a tall feed silo, the pig overlapping the base of the barn; a medium hen with two small chicks pecking on a curved grassy slope to one side; a simple wooden fence line running in a gentle curve across the whole frame as the connective element; the ground is the curve of the slope, never a flat line.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #7d8a4e, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: cages, crates, bars, chains, slaughterhouse, knives, meat, plates, burgers, blood, sad or crying animals, cartoon smiling animal faces, protest signs, crossed-out symbols, warning signs, price tags, test tubes or lab meat, a cow as the main figure, smokestacks, a farmer or any person.
