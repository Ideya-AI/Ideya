## Notes for the human
- could-a-planned-reduction-in-rich (question 8, detail): since question 5 dropped its mention, question 8 is the only place the review appears, and it is introduced as "the Savin and van den Bergh review" as if the reader had already met it. The reader gets no year, scope or link. The page's only disclosure that van den Bergh originated agrowth sits under the EU survey stat. The sentence is in the camp's voice, so nothing is misattributed, but "a 2024 review by Ivan Savin and Jeroen van den Bergh" would give the reader a foothold.
- Carried from the first audit and still open:
  - how-much-growth-is-it-worth (question 6, why): the line that cost-benefit models "often favor slower cuts than the Paris goals" sits beside stat 5's IPCC line that the benefit of limiting warming to 2°C exceeds the cost in most of the literature. Both can be true, but a reader is left unsure what the economics says about Paris.
  - yearly-emissions-cuts-achieved-versus-needed (stat 6): the bar label "Needed by 2025" reads like a deadline that has already passed. The "UK achieved" bar is not explained.
  - fall-in-uk-emissions-by-accounting (stat 7): "Territorial" and "Production-based" are not distinguished. The closing DESNZ 2024 sentence has a different baseline and no link.
  - climate-policy-researchers-who-prefer-agrowth (stat 13): the detail credits The Conversation, but the link goes to the UAB press release.
  - Stats 3 and 4 give 38.1 and 38.4 billion tonnes for 2025 emissions. These are different measures (GCP fossil CO2 against IEA energy CO2).
  - The summary, question 1 and the crux lead are nearly the same sentence.
  - "Post-growth" is claimed in both position 2's detail and position 3's.
  - Actions: the growth-first camp has one tagged action and the others have two each. The first six actions cover all four camps.
  - Cadence: most stat details close with "Green growth supporters...; degrowth supporters...".
  - Layout: lean labels wrap at 390 (Q2, Q3, Q7, Q8). Some bar-row labels wrap at 1440. The longest position name wraps as an action tag at 390. Nothing is clipped.
  - Template: with 15 stats, the last half-width tile sits alone on its row at 1440 when expanded.
