## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a tall wooden signpost with two arrow boards pointing different ways stands at a fork in a path as the dominant focal mass; a row of standing dominoes runs along the near path toward the fork, the nearest domino overlapping the foot of the signpost; the path splits into two branches that wind away over rolling hills and cross the whole frame as the connective element; a small pocket watch lies open on the grass in the foreground at the smallest scale; a medium tree on a far hill behind one branch; the ground is a gentle rolling hill curve.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b8653f, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: puppets, marionette strings, chains, shackles, cages, prison bars, gavels, scales of justice, brains, neurons, dice, coins, question marks, crossroads with people, human figures, hands, faces, eyes, toppling or falling dominoes, arrows labeled with words, text, letters on signs, halos, devils, angels, crossed-out symbols, check marks.
