## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a compact scene sitting on a small rounded hill, the hill's curve ending well inside the frame on both sides: a large domed metal lunch pail with a handle as the dominant focal mass at center; a work hard hat resting on top of the lunch pail, overlapping its lid; a plain paper pay envelope leaning against the pail's side at small scale; a small warehouse with a roll-up door and a small delivery scooter with a cargo box on the crest behind at medium scale; a line of utility poles with wires running across the hill behind everything as the connective element, starting and ending inside the frame.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b48f2c, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: picket signs, raised fists, protest banners, strikers, megaphones, handshakes, chains, broken chains, dollar signs, currency symbols, banknotes, money bags, top hats, briefcases, factory smokestacks, charts, graphs, arrows, scales of justice, gears, tug of war, contrast of light and dark halves.
