## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: A large blister pack of tablets, standing upright and tilted slightly, is the dominant focal mass on a softly curving hill. A small blank paper price tag on a string hangs from one corner of the blister pack, overlapping its edge. To the left, a medium laboratory microscope stands beside a short stack of three plain coins with no markings. A winding path crosses the hill behind them, leading to a small pharmacy storefront with a plain cross sign at the right. Big blister pack, medium microscope and pharmacy, small coins and tag.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b0567f, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: dollar signs, currency symbols, numbers or text on the tag, money bags, piles of cash, scales of justice, broken or crossed-out pills, coffins, sad or sick people, hands, factory smokestacks, arrows, upward charts, red ink, second color
