## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: one compact scene sitting on a single small rounded island mound, outlined and filled, whose curved edges slope down and end well inside the frame on both sides, never a flat line: a small simple house raised high on tall stilts as the dominant focal mass, filled, standing on the crest of the mound; a short low wall of stacked sandbags of medium size curving along the mound's slope in front of the stilts, overlapping their feet, filled; a slim water-depth gauge post with evenly spaced tick marks, filled, standing at the right foot of the mound, the smallest element; a round-crowned tree behind the house's left side, partly hidden by it; one long gentle wavy water line crossing the whole scene in front of the mound's lower slopes and the gauge post as the connective element, starting and ending inside the frame.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #3f9a6a, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: no storm clouds, no lightning, no rain, no hurricane spiral, no submerged or wrecked houses, no people, no rescue boats, no dollar signs or money, no insurance documents, no government buildings, no thermometer, no globe, no melting ice, no factory smoke, no arrows
