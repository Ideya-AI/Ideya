## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a large round sphere made of connected network nodes and short links, rising like a full moon over a gently curved horizon as the dominant focal mass; a small city skyline of six simple buildings along the curved ground in front, overlapping the lower edge of the sphere; a line of power pylons with wires running from the city across the whole frame toward the sphere as the connective element; one medium rounded tree at the right edge of the city; the ground is a wide gentle curve like the edge of a planet.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #3b7ea1, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: robots, humanoid machines, glowing red eyes, skulls, mushroom clouds, explosions, fire, cracks in the globe, broken or burning cities, warning triangles, exclamation marks, countdown clocks, biohazard symbols, chains, cages, padlocks, puppet strings, brains, rockets, flags, protest signs.
