## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: A broad old oak tree as the dominant mass, standing on a gently rounded hill. A young sapling tied to a wooden stake in front of the oak, overlapping the base of its trunk. A small watering can beside the sapling. A low rising sun, half above a long distant horizon line that runs across the whole frame behind the hill. Two or three tiny birds in the sky.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b8653f, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: people, hands, faces, babies, cradles, footprints, roads or winding paths, globes or planet Earth, clocks, mushroom clouds, asteroids, skulls, smokestacks, flames, dollar signs or coins, scales of justice, text or numbers, a frame or border, a second color
