## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: one compact school scene grouped in the center with wide white margins, nothing touching the edges: a large classic school bus seen from the side as the dominant focal mass, filled, driving along an open road that sweeps in a gentle S-curve across the whole foreground from lower left to the right, the road's ends simply stopping short of the margins, never closing into a loop or ring; a medium traditional schoolhouse with a small bell tower on a rounded hill behind the bus, the bus overlapping the foot of the hill, filled; a small school of a different shape, a low modern building with a flat roof and a row of windows, on a second, farther hill to the right, filled; one narrow lane branches off the road and winds up to each school; a small school backpack resting on the grass beside the road in front of the bus; the ground is two overlapping gentle rounded hills, filled.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #9459a2, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: dollar signs, banknotes, coins, money bags, piggy banks, vouchers, tickets, price tags, signposts, road signs, arrows, ladders, cracks, crumbling or boarded-up buildings, locked gates, fences, padlocks, crosses, steeples, churches, flags, protest signs, balance scales, question marks, charts, graphs, children, students, teachers, crowds, faces, apples, chalkboards with writing, clocks, bicycles, swings, kites.
