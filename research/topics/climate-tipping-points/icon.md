## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: one compact coastal scene grouped in the center with wide white margins, nothing touching the edges: a tall ice sheet with a sheer flat-topped front edge as the dominant focal mass on the left half, filled; a medium iceberg floating in front of its foot, overlapping the ice face, filled above and below the waterline; a low rounded hill of rainforest tree crowns rising behind the right side of the ice sheet, partly hidden by it; a small rounded coral clump on the curved sea floor at the lower right, below the water; one long wavy sea surface line crossing the whole scene through the iceberg as the connective element; the sea floor is a gentle curve.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #3f9a6a, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: thermometers, melting globes, polar bears, flames, wildfire, smokestacks, dripping or melting ice, cracks, warning signs, arrows, dials or gauges, seesaws, dominoes, balls on hills, clocks, dead trees, bleached skeletons, storms, sun.
