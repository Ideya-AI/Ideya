## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a tall maize plant with broad leaves and one husked ear as the dominant focal mass, rising from the crest of a single rounded hill of crop rows at the center-left; a small simple DNA double-helix with filled ribbons beside the upper stalk, overlapping one leaf; a medium glass research greenhouse with a filled interior sitting on the far right shoulder of the hill; a small filled seed sack at the foot of the maize plant; a gently curving farm track running across the whole hill from left to right as the connective element; the hill is a soft filled mound whose curved edges end well inside the frame, with white space all around, never a flat line and never running off the edges.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #7d8a4e, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: syringes or needles, injected fruit, a skull, warning or biohazard signs, a "frankenfood" monster or stitched vegetable, a test tube or flask poured onto crops, glowing or mutant plants, crossed-out symbols, price tags, padlocks or chains on seeds, corporate logos, a lab coat or scientist, tractors spraying, wheat sheaves, globes, labels with writing.
