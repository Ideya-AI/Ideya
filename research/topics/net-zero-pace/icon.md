## Scene
A suburban house sits on a curved green hill as the dominant mass, its roof covered in solar panels, a heat pump unit beside its wall and an electric car parked at a small charger in front, the car overlapping the house corner. Behind the house on a farther hill stand a medium factory chimney with a soft pale plume and two small wind turbines. Power lines run from the distant hill across the whole frame down to the house.

## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: A suburban house sits on a curved green hill as the dominant mass, its roof covered in solar panels, a heat pump unit beside its wall and an electric car parked at a small charger in front, the car overlapping the house corner. Behind the house on a farther hill stand a medium factory chimney with a soft pale plume and two small wind turbines. Power lines run from the distant hill across the whole frame down to the house.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #3f9a6a, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: clocks, hourglasses, countdown timers, calendars, the numbers 2050 or any year, thermometers, flames, burning or melting globes, dollar signs or coins, up or down arrows, finish lines or flags, checkered flags, speedometers, traffic lights, stop signs, dark smoke, smog, dead trees, cracked earth, a balance scale, a split or mirrored composition
