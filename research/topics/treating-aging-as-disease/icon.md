## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: A large round slice of a tree trunk stands upright on its edge as the clearly dominant mass, its face showing many plain concentric growth rings inside a thin bark rim, the rings softly filled. A large medicine capsule leans against the lower right of the wood slice, overlapping it. They stand on a low, gently rounded mound of ground only a little wider than the group, whose curved outline ends softly inside the frame on both sides. A small laboratory flask stands on the mound's left side at the medium scale with a tiny medicine bottle beside it at the smallest scale. A short winding path with a soft fill runs from the front edge of the mound past the capsule to its crest.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b0567f, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: clocks with hands, numbers or digits, birthday candles, gravestones, skulls, wilting flowers, canes, walkers, wheelchairs, rocking chairs, wrinkled faces, human figures or silhouettes, faces, heartbeat lines, infinity signs, fountains, syringes, dollar signs, warning signs, crossed out symbols, a second color
