# Icon brief — Abortion (moral status of the unborn)

Deliverable: `static/img/topics/moral-status-of-the-unborn.png`, 1035×690 on white.

## Scene

A clinic ultrasound cart is the dominant mass, standing right of center with a large
rounded monitor whose screen holds only an abstract wedge-shaped scan cone with three
plain sweep arcs inside it and no object of any kind within the cone; its handheld probe hangs on a coiled cord that crosses in front of the cart's
base. Behind and overlapping the cart's left edge stands a tall wall calendar, its grid
of week squares partly hidden by the monitor. Low and small at the front sits a closed
statute book with a ribbon marker, overlapping the cart's wheels. A small domed
legislative building sits far back on the left at the end of a curving path that sweeps
out of the lower left corner, past the book, and behind the cart, over a gently rounded
ground mound rather than a flat line. A thin horizon curve runs edge to edge behind
everything.

## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: A clinic ultrasound cart is the dominant mass, standing right of center with a large rounded monitor whose screen holds only an abstract wedge-shaped scan cone with three plain sweep arcs inside it and no object of any kind within the cone; its handheld probe hangs on a coiled cord that crosses in front of the cart's base. Behind and overlapping the cart's left edge stands a tall wall calendar, its grid of week squares partly hidden by the monitor. Low and small at the front sits a closed statute book with a ribbon marker, overlapping the cart's wheels. A small domed legislative building sits far back on the left at the end of a curving path that sweeps out of the lower left corner, past the book, and behind the cart, over a gently rounded ground mound rather than a flat line. A thin horizon curve runs edge to edge behind everything.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b8653f, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: no coat hangers, no protest signs or placards, no slogans or lettering of any kind, no hearts, no halos, no crosses, no praying hands, no fetus or embryo or infant outline anywhere, nothing figurative inside the scan cone, no cradled or held fetus, no clasped adult hands, no people or faces or body parts, no blood, no scales of justice, no gavel, no ribbons or awareness symbols, no arrows, no mirrored or symmetrical two-sided layout, no speech bubbles, no frame or border, no second hue, no shading gradients
