about, keeps its own voice out of the fight, and builds one argument from the
numbers through the questions to a crux that follows from them: eighteen stats that

## Notes for the human
- Stat "US states by what their abortion law permits": the note says "nine plus DC
  set no limit" while the detail says "The District of Columbia is counted with the
  nine states that set no gestational limit," and the chart row reads 9. One
  jurisdiction either way, and the reading of the section does not change, but the
  glance and the expanded layer contradict each other in the same tile. Dropping
  "plus DC" from the note would settle it.
- Stat "US abortions by 13 weeks of gestation", detail: "the disputed entity is
  between the size of a lentil and a lime." The size simile is the one place the
  page's own voice reaches for an image that does rhetorical work for one side — a
  camp that locates status at fertilization holds that size is exactly what does not
  matter. Stating the gestational size plainly, or cutting the clause, would keep
  the sentence's point without the nudge.
- Question "Should viability be the line the law uses?": the lean labels are "The
  viability line" and "A fixed line," but the detail ends by saying rejecters split
  between people who would set an earlier fixed limit and people who would set none.
  A reader who rejects viability because they want no limit has nowhere to lean.
- Repetition worth a second look, none of it verbatim: the list of candidate
  thresholds (implantation, primitive streak, cardiac activity, brain activity,
  sentience, viability, birth) and the "each line excludes beings its proposer wants
  included" move appear in both question 1's detail and position 3's detail; the
  bodily-autonomy rebuttal (the dependent was brought into being by the woman's own
  act, in the only place it can survive, and abortion ends rather than detaches)
  appears in question 2's detail and again in position 3's detail in nearly the same
  order. The November 2026 ballot appears in why-now, question 7, position 4 and an
  action — each time doing different work, but four times.
- Actions tilt slightly by count: "Leave the decision to the pregnant woman" carries
  three cards (Thomson, AbortionFinder, National Network of Abortion Funds) against
  two for "Protect unborn human life from conception" and one each for the rest. The
  local and donate pairs are matched one-for-one, so the balance holds where it is
  most visible; only the reading list is lopsided.
- Crux values panel ends "A society can decide this. It cannot discover it." Read in
  context it is scoped to measurement by the sentence before it, but on this topic
  one camp's whole claim is that moral status is a fact to be recognized rather than
  chosen. Worth a second read to confirm it lands as scoped.
- Layout, carried over from the views stage and confirmed: at 390 the lean labels
  "Dependency obliges" (Q2) and "Not before 28 weeks" (Q5) each wrap to two lines
  inside their buttons, and the position chip "Reduce abortion by removing the
  reasons women seek it" wraps to two lines in the Option Line action card. All
  contained, none clipped.
