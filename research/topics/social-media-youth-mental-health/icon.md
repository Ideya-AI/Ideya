## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a large smartphone standing upright as the dominant focal mass, its screen showing a few simple rounded app tiles and a small heart badge; a child's school backpack of medium size leaning against the foot of the phone so it overlaps it; a small playground swing set on the hill behind at the right; a small bicycle low at the left; the ground is the rounded curve of a grassy hill; a winding footpath runs across the whole frame from the bicycle past the phone to the swings as the connective element.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #9459a2, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: sad or crying faces, emoji faces, chains, locks, cages, prison bars, skulls, broken or cracked screens, warning or hazard signs, red notification alarms, storm clouds, dark gloomy tones, tangled cords around anything, brain illustrations, pills, thumbs-down icons, app logos or brand marks, human figures or hands.
