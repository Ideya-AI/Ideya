## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: A large glass donation jar half full of coins as the dominant focal mass in the foreground, two coins falling into its slot. Behind and overlapping it, a small village of three simple houses with a schoolhouse and a hand water pump on a rounded hill. A winding road runs from the jar across the rolling ground to the village and on to a distant second hill with a tiny clinic building under a curving horizon. A mosquito net hung over a small cot sits beside the pump, small scale.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b8653f, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: hearts, hands, people, faces, dollar or currency signs, text or numbers, scales of justice, charts or graphs, protest signs or banners, money bags, government buildings, trophies, a frame or border, a second color
