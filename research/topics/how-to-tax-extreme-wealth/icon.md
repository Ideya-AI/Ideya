## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: A large open bank vault door, round and heavy, standing slightly ajar as the dominant mass at center left. A small stack of share certificates with ribbon seals and a folded property deed overlap the vault door's lower edge. A tax form on a clipboard with blank lines and a pen leans against the certificates. A rounded hill curves across the ground, and a winding path crosses the frame from the vault up the hill to a small grand manor house with columns on the crest at right.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b48f2c, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: no money bags, no dollar signs or currency symbols, no top hats or monocles, no coin towers, no balance scales, no guillotines or pitchforks, no scissors or hands grabbing, no arrows or charts, no flags, no people or faces, no text, letters or numbers, no frame or border, no second color
