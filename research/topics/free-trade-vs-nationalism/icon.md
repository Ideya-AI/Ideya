## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a large container ship loaded with neat rows of ribbed shipping containers as the dominant focal mass, sailing on a calm gently curving sea swell; a medium dockside gantry crane on a rounded shore at the right, its boom reaching out over the ship's bow and overlapping it; a small factory with a sawtooth roof on the shore behind the crane; a small freight truck carrying one container on a road that curves from the factory down to the quay; the curving shoreline and road together run across the frame as the connective element; the ground is a soft rounded rise meeting the water in a curve.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b48f2c, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: flags, national symbols, maps, globes, walls, fences, gates, barriers, padlocks, chains, scales of justice, money or coin symbols, dollar signs, percent signs, arrows, charts, smoke, storm waves, cracked or broken objects, people.
