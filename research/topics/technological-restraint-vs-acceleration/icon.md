## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: A large square microchip with pins on all four sides stands as the dominant mass, tilted slightly, on a gently rounded hill; a medium car speedometer dial with a plain needle overlaps the chip's lower right corner; a medium gear sits behind the chip's left edge; a small atom and a small DNA helix float in the upper sky; a winding road runs from the lower left across the hill and over the horizon to the right, crossing the whole frame.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b3404f, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: robots, humanoid figures, glowing brains, skulls, explosions, mushroom clouds, stop signs, red lights, warning triangles, chains, padlocks, fire, rockets launching, upward arrows, rising charts, gas and brake pedals, ruins, Terminator eyes, any text or numbers on the dial
