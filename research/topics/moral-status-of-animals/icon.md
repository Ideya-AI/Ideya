## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a large calm deer standing in profile on a meadow as the dominant focal mass; a seated dog in the foreground overlapping the deer's legs, looking the same way; a small hen at the dog's side at the smallest scale; a small house with a pitched roof and a low fence on a far hill behind; a winding stream that runs across the whole frame from the house to the foreground as the connective element; the ground is a gentle rolling hill curve.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b8653f, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: cages, chains, bars, fences enclosing animals, slaughterhouses, factory barns, meat, bones, knives, forks, plates, laboratory flasks or syringes, lab mice, hunting rifles, traps, protest signs, banners, hearts, halos, tears, scales of justice, gavels, human figures, hands, faces with expressions, text, crossed-out symbols, check marks.
