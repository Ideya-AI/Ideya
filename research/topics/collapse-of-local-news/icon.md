## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: one compact small-town scene grouped in the center with wide white margins, nothing touching the edges: a large folded newspaper standing upright on its edge as the dominant focal mass, its front page showing only a blank masthead band, one plain picture box and a few blank ruled column lines, no letters; behind it and partly overlapped by it, a small town hall of medium size with a little clock tower and a plain front door; a small reporter's spiral notepad with a pencil lying at the newspaper's foot, overlapping its lower corner; a low wooden utility pole at one side with a single power line drooping across the frame behind the town hall; all of it sitting on one gently rounded filled hill whose curve ends well inside the frame on both sides.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #2f8f86, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: any letters or words or headlines or a masthead name, torn or crumpled or burning newspapers, tombstones, gravestones, wilting flowers, for-sale or closed signs, boarded-up windows, cobwebs, dollar signs, tech company logos, robots, smartphones crushing paper, megaphones, protest signs, people or faces, a frame or border around the scene
