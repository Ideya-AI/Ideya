## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a large faceted chunk of raw ore with a few crystal points as the dominant focal mass in the foreground; behind it a terraced open-pit mine of medium size cut into a rounded hill, its stepped benches overlapping the ore's back edge; a small haul truck on one of the benches; a conveyor belt on slim legs running diagonally across the whole frame from the pit down to a small upright cylindrical battery cell at the lower right as the connective element; the ground is a gentle rolling curve.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #3f9a6a, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: flags, national symbols, maps, dragons, children or workers, chains, shackles, skulls, smoke, dust clouds, money or coin symbols, cracked earth, polluted water, gold bars, padlocks, arrows, warning signs.
