## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a tall glass vaccine vial with a rubber-stoppered cap as the dominant focal mass, standing in the foreground; a capped syringe lying diagonally in front of the vial's base, overlapping it; a small schoolhouse with a bell tower on a hill behind at a smaller scale; a winding path that runs from the schoolhouse across the whole frame to the vial as the connective element; the ground is a gentle rolling hill curve, with one small rounded tree beside the school.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b0567f, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: virus particles or spiky germs, face masks, skulls, warning signs, hazard symbols, padlocks, chains, gavels, handcuffs, shields, flags, the red cross emblem, hospital buildings, check marks or X marks, crossed-out symbols, dripping needles, needles pointing at anything, arms or bodies, crowds, protest signs, documents with text.
