## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a small border checkpoint booth with a flat roof and one window as the dominant focal mass; a long striped boom barrier arm pivoting from a post beside the booth, raised halfway at a slant, overlapping the booth's front; a road that curves unbroken from the lower left, passes under the barrier arm and winds away to the upper right as the connective element; a medium closed suitcase with a handle standing at the roadside near the post; a small cluster of distant rooftops on the far rise; the ground is the gentle curve of a low rise.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #6a62b0, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: walls, fences, barbed wire, razor wire, cages, chains, padlocks, handcuffs, soldiers, guns, watchtowers, searchlights, flags, national symbols, the Statue of Liberty, open arms, hands, welcome signs, stop signs, arrows, crowded boats, lines of people, crowds, maps with country outlines, a row of doors.
