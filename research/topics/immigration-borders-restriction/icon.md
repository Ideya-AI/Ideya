# Immigration: Open Borders vs. Restriction — primary icon art brief

Handoff brief for an illustrator or image/vector tool. Full style theme: `docs/DESIGN.md` (Topic art).
Template siblings: `nuclear-energy.md` (the tuned reference),
`democratic-backsliding-populism.md` — the other **Governance & Political Order** topic,
the gov-purple reference and this icon's nearest neighbor on the wall —
`frontier-ai-regulation.md` and `vaccine-mandates.md` (the object-only still-life form),
and `economic-inequality.md` / `social-media-youth-mental-health.md` (current house
weight, and the source of the no-figures rule).

**No human figures anywhere in this scene.** Two reasons, and both are hard. The
`economic-inequality` round measured the generator drawing repeated figures at unequal
sizes across all ten candidates and refusing to correct them (see that brief's Notes).
And on *this* topic a drawn person is never neutral: one figure at a border is either a
supplicant or an intruder, and several are a crowd, a queue, or a flood. The migrant is
present as what they carry, never as a body.

## Use
The topic's **primary hero image**. Shows large on the topic page, poster, and social
cards; also reduced into a rounded-square tinted tile and shown on a dark band. Must
read both large and small, on light and dark.

## Style (non-negotiable)
- **Minimal monoline** — ONE consistent stroke weight across the whole image, rounded
  caps/joins. References: **Gorges Beer Co** menu icons, **Flaticon "Lineal."**
- **Duotone, single color, two tones** — line at full strength + a soft (~16–18%) fill
  of the **same hue** on the masses. No second color, no gradients, no shadows.
- **Color:** the category color (Governance & Political Order = **gov purple**,
  `#7a6aa8`). Deliver single-color so it can be recolored per category.
- Flat, clean, editorial.
- **Filled masses, not linework-only** — lesson from earlier topics: thin unfilled
  linework nearly vanished at 40px. **Every** major element in this scene is a filled
  shape carrying the duotone tint; nothing important may be a bare line. The door frame
  in particular is drawn as narrow filled bars, never as a hairline rectangle.

## Composition
A small bespoke **scene** combining several *simple* sub-icons in one frame — about
**2.5–3.5× the complexity of a single Gorges icon**. Generate wide (3:2); framing is
normalized in post (trim to content, ~8% pad, fit 3:2 on white). Legible from ~40px
to hero size, readable on a dark background.

An **object-only still life** on one ground line: three objects, the tall one centered
and the two low ones flanking it. The silhouette is **one tall open frame between two
low masses**, which is what separates it at tile size from `vaccine-mandates` (a tall
*peaked* mass off to one side) and from `frontier-ai-regulation` (all-low). It must also
not echo the other gov-purple tile: `democratic-backsliding-populism` is a peopled,
facing-each-other scene with a columned building; this one has no figures, no building,
and no two sides.

**The flanking objects are placed symmetrically and neither one is going anywhere.**
That is deliberate. A bag set down beside a door reads as a bag; a bag angled toward the
door reads as arrival or expulsion, and the picture would have answered the question.

## Elements — promise *and* peril
The debate is how open a state should be — freedom of movement and the economic gains
from it against sovereignty, cohesion, wages, asylum duties, and what emigration does to
origin countries. Admission is claimed as a right migrants hold, a bargain states strike,
and a sovereign choice; the fight is over who the border serves. The art must hold that
open, and must neither welcome nor warn:
- **A plain freestanding doorway standing at the center on the ground line** — the focal
  mass and the threshold itself, echoing the topic's Phosphor `door-open` glyph. Two
  upright jambs, a flat head, and a threshold sill along the bottom, all drawn as narrow
  **filled bars**. **Freestanding: no wall, fence, hedge, gate post, or barrier attached
  to either side** — a threshold is a decision, a wall is a verdict. Nothing is visible
  through the empty part of the opening: no landscape, no beam of light, no glow, no
  second ground beyond it. There is no near side and no far side.
- **A plain door leaf inside the frame, hinged at one jamb and standing half open** —
  drawn flat, in the same plane as the frame, as a simple filled panel covering about
  half the opening's width and leaving the other half clear. **Half is the neutrality
  device**: *how* open is the question, so the door is set at neither extreme, the way
  `economic-inequality`'s policy lever stands centered in its travel. A door thrown wide
  argues one case and a door shut, locked, barred, or chained argues the other. Filled
  mass.
- **A plain suitcase standing upright on the ground line, to one side of the doorway** —
  the migrant, present as what they carry, since no person may be drawn. A wide rounded
  rectangle body with one short handle on the top edge and a single plain horizontal band
  across the front. **Set down, level, square to the viewer, at rest** — not tilted, not
  open, not spilling, not roped, not stacked, not carried. About half the doorway's
  height, a clear gap away from it, and **not touching, entering, or leaning toward** the
  door. Filled mass.
- **A plain rubber stamp standing upright at rest on the same ground line, on the other
  side of the doorway** — sovereign discretion, drawn as the instrument rather than the
  act, the way `frontier-ai-regulation` lays its gavel down and `vaccine-mandates` leaves
  its form unsigned. A wide flat rectangular base block on the ground, a short narrow neck
  rising from its center, and a plain rounded dome handle on top. **Standing still and
  upright — never pressed down, never held, never mid-strike**, with no ink pad, no ink
  mark, no impression on the ground or on anything else, and no motion lines. Who picks it
  up, on whose behalf, and by what right is the topic; drawing it in nobody's hand is how
  the scene declines to say. Roughly the suitcase's height, so the two flanks balance.
  Filled mass.
- **A single straight horizontal ground line across the scene** — one plane under the
  suitcase, the doorway, and the stamp alike (mirrors the sibling compositions): the
  traveler, the threshold, and the authority all stand on the same earth, which is most
  of the argument.

## Mood
Neutral and editorial — a door standing half open on open ground, a bag set down beside
it, and the stamp that decides standing in nobody's hand. Whether admission is a right, a
bargain, or a sovereign choice, and how far the door should swing, are the open questions
the scene holds. Not a welcome, not a warning; not a border scene, not a homecoming.

## Avoid
Photorealism; more than one color; gradients/shadows; **human figures of any kind** — no
migrant, official, guard, family, child, crowd, queue, silhouette, face, or hand, and no
repeated figures in particular (measured failure, see `economic-inequality.md`); **a wall,
fence, border barrier, barbed or razor wire, bollards, a checkpoint booth, a watchtower,
a boom gate, a turnstile, or a security camera** (verdict: the border is a fortress); a
door that is **shut, locked, bolted, chained, barred, padlocked, boarded, crossed out or
slashed** (verdict: closed), and equally a door **flung fully wide, with a welcome mat,
open arms, a light beam, rays, sparkles, or an inviting landscape beyond it** (verdict:
open); any **arrow, chevron, dashed or dotted trail, footprints, tire tracks, a path, or a
queue rope** anywhere — direction of travel is a verdict about flow, in either direction;
boats, rafts, dinghies, life jackets, oceans, deserts, caravans, bundles on poles, or any
humanitarian tableau (one camp's imagery); handcuffs, cages, bars, detention, a dropped
toy, or an abandoned shoe (advocacy); a "no entry" sign, stop sign, prohibition circle, or
red slash; a check mark, cross, tick, seal, or stamped impression on any surface; passport
stamps, ID cards, or fingerprints; a globe with converging lines, a world map, a compass, a
migration map, or any country's outline; **flags, national colors, eagles, government
seals, uniforms, badges, or the Statue of Liberty**; a rising or falling line chart, bar
chart, population graph, axis, tick marks, or percent signs (verdict: the numbers settle
it); a broken, tipped, torn, or spilled suitcase, or a stack or pile of luggage (verdict:
harm, or "flood"); scales, gavels, or ballot boxes (they belong to the neighboring tiles);
fussy internal detail (keyholes, hinges, handle plates, panel mouldings on the door, brick
or grain texture, luggage stickers, straps, tags, wheels, or a telescoping handle,
screws or knurling on the stamp); clutter; anything that reads as pro- or anti-immigration.

## Deliverable
Ideally **vector (SVG)**, single-color, transparent ground. PNG accepted
(the pipeline is raster-primary).

## Generation prompt (Nano Banana / Gemini)
**Frameless** — the art has NO drawn border. The CSS tile renders the rounded-square
frame. Lessons carried over from the earlier generations: force the soft fill to
~15–18% (it renders near-invisible if unstressed), force uniform stroke weight (thin
secondary elements drift thinner), and make every major element a **filled mass** —
earlier linework-only elements nearly vanished at 40px. New this topic: a door frame is
the classic hairline-rectangle trap, so its jambs, head and sill are specified as filled
bars; and the model will try to complete a border scene with a wall, a fence, a flag, or a
figure, so the negative list is long and load-bearing.

**Notes:**
- Over-generate on the **half-open door leaf** and the **freestanding frame**. The leaf is
  the neutrality device and it drifts to both extremes: it renders as a fully shut slab
  (verdict: closed) or vanishes entirely into an empty archway (verdict: open). Half the
  opening filled, half clear, is the target — measure the leaf against the opening rather
  than eyeballing it. The frame wants to grow a wall, a fence, or a fragment of masonry on
  one or both sides; the moment it does, the picture is about a barrier, and the candidate
  is out.
- Watch for the generator completing a **border scene** — wire, a boom gate, a guard
  booth, a watchtower, a flag on a pole, an eagle, or a uniformed figure. Any one of them
  takes a side in the topic's central fight and disqualifies the candidate outright.
- Watch for **direction of travel**. An arrow, a dotted trail, footprints, or the suitcase
  tipped or angled toward the doorway converts a still life into a story about arrival or
  removal. The suitcase must sit square, level, and clear of the frame.
- Watch for the **stamp reading as something else** — it wants to become a lamp, a
  mushroom, a bell, a plunger, or a gavel. The shape is base block, short neck, dome
  handle, standing upright. A gavel in particular is wrong twice: it is the wrong
  instrument and it belongs to `frontier-ai-regulation` on the same wall.
- Watch for the **door frame rendering as hairlines**. A stroked rectangle with a white
  interior is the classic 40px failure on this composition — the jambs, head, and sill must
  be filled bars carrying the tint.
- Watch for the door drifting into being the whole picture — the Phosphor `door-open` chip
  already carries the bare-door idea, and the raster icon has to read as a scene, not as a
  bigger version of the glyph.
- Watch for outline-only elements — every major mass must carry the purple fill; a
  candidate whose suitcase or door leaf renders as bare linework fails the 40px test.
- White background is intentional — the primary sits on a neutral tile; the frame
  comes from the tile, not the art.
- After generating: save as `static/img/topics/immigration-borders-restriction.png`,
  set `Topic.primary_art = "img/topics/immigration-borders-restriction.png"`, and
  regenerate the social PNGs
  (`render_social --slug immigration-borders-restriction --formats og,sq,pt`).
