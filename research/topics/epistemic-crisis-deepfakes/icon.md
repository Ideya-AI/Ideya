## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a large rounded video player window seen from the front as the dominant focal mass, its screen showing a simple landscape picture of a mountain peak and a round sun, with a plain triangular play button and a thin progress bar along its bottom; a second, slightly smaller copy of the same picture frame sits offset behind it to the upper left, overlapping, its mountain drawn a little shifted so the two pictures almost but not quite match; a small four-pointed sparkle star sits at the upper right corner of the front window; the window rests on a low rounded mound, outlined and softly filled; one long thin flowing ribbon of dashed signal line runs across the whole width of the frame behind the mound as the connective element, passing through two small dots; a small cloud at the upper right.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #2f8f86, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning.
