## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a large smartphone as the dominant focal mass, standing upright, outlined and softly filled, its screen showing a simple feed with one plain advertisement card marked only by a small shopping-bag shape; a medium server cabinet with three stacked drawers and small round lights stands to the right, partly behind the phone; one long curved stream of small round data dots flows from the phone's screen in a gentle arc across the frame into the server; a small cursor arrow and a small location pin float near the dots; everything sits on a low rounded mound, outlined and softly filled; everything flat, no drop shadow.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #2f8f86, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: people, hands, faces, eyes, eyeballs, text, letters, numbers, code, binary digits, logos, brand marks, dollar signs, coins, money bags, cookies, fishhooks, puppet strings, surveillance cameras, magnifying glasses, padlocks, broken locks, chains, cages, shields, skulls, warning triangles, exclamation marks, red marks, octopus, tentacles, vacuum cleaners, question marks.
