## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a large house key standing upright as the dominant focal mass, its round bow at the top; the bow of the key frames a small pitched-roof house drawn inside it; a medium apartment block with a simple window grid at the right, overlapped by the key shaft; a small tower crane far behind on the rise; one road curving unbroken across the whole frame from the lower left, up past the base of the key and behind the apartment block as the connective element; the ground is the gentle curve of a low rise.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b48f2c, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: dollar signs, currency symbols, price tags, for-sale signs, sold signs, banknotes, piggy banks, hands, arrows going up or down, graphs, padlocks, chains, cages, tents, homeless figures, eviction notices, a row of identical small houses.

## Selection
Round 1, three scenes at two samples each: A house with key leaning on it (r1-a, r1-b), B apartment block under construction with crane (r1-c, r1-d), C upright key whose bow frames a house (r1-e, r1-f). $0.80.
Picked r1-f: the key is one strong silhouette that reads as housing at tile size, apartment block overlaps it, compact and distinct from the UBI mailbox and row of houses. B leaned toward the build-more position by making construction the subject; A was plain but its house and key sat side by side; r1-e ran the road off the frame edge with a weak ground.
Round 2, one edit on r1-f (crane strokes thinner than the rest, a stray third line in the road, the left rise unfilled). $0.27, total $1.07. Neither beat the incumbent: r2-a lost the key fill, r2-b added a hard-edged fill block on the left, road unchanged. Kept r1-f.
Normalized r1-f; the 40px thumbnail reads as a key with a building. Glyph: house (no other topic in economy uses it).
