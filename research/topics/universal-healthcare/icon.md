## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a broad two-story clinic building with a plain cross on its facade as the dominant focal mass; a small ambulance parked in front, overlapping the building's lower corner; a cluster of three small houses on the far left at a smaller scale; a winding road that runs from the houses across the whole frame to the clinic's door as the connective element; the ground is a gentle rolling hill curve, with one rounded tree of medium size beside the clinic.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b0567f, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: dollar signs, coins, price tags, bills or invoices, piggy banks, flags, the caduceus, the red cross emblem in red, hospital beds, syringes, skulls, protest signs, queues of people, broken or crumbling buildings, padlocks, scales of justice.
