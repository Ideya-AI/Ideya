## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: one compact home scene grouped in the center with wide white margins, nothing touching the edges: a simple two-story family house with a pitched roof as the dominant focal mass, filled; a medium baby pram with a rounded hood standing in front of the house, overlapping its lower corner, filled; a small wooden cradle on rockers beside the pram; a small rounded tree behind the right side of the house, partly hidden by it; one long clothesline strung across the whole scene from the house to the tree, with a few tiny baby garments hanging from it, as the connective element; the ground is a gentle rounded hill.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #9459a2, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning.
