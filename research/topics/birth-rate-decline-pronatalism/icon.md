# Birth-Rate Decline & Pronatalism — primary icon art brief

Handoff brief for an illustrator or image/vector tool. Full style theme: `docs/DESIGN.md` (Topic art).
Template siblings: `nuclear-energy.md` (the tuned reference), `climate-tipping-points.md`,
`content-moderation-free-speech.md`, `housing-affordability-crisis.md`.
First topic in **Society, Culture & Identity** — sets the olive reference.

## Use
The topic's **primary hero image**. Shows large on the topic page, poster, and social
cards; also reduced into a rounded-square tinted tile and shown on a dark band. Must
read both large and small, on light and dark.

## Style (non-negotiable)
- **Minimal monoline** — ONE consistent stroke weight across the whole image, rounded
  caps/joins. References: **Gorges Beer Co** menu icons, **Flaticon "Lineal."**
- **Duotone, single color, two tones** — line at full strength + a soft (~16–18%) fill
  of the **same hue** on the masses. No second color, no gradients, no shadows.
- **Color:** the category color (Society, Culture & Identity = **society olive**,
  `#7d8a4e`). Deliver single-color so it can be recolored per category.
- Flat, clean, editorial.
- **Filled masses, not linework-only** — lesson from earlier topics: thin unfilled
  curves nearly vanished at 40px. Every major element in this scene is a filled
  shape carrying the duotone tint; nothing important may be a bare line.

## Composition
A small bespoke **scene** combining several *simple* sub-icons in one frame — about
**2.5–3.5× the complexity of a single Gorges icon**. Generate wide (3:2); framing is
normalized in post (trim to content, ~8% pad, fit 3:2 on white). Legible from ~40px
to hero size, readable on a dark background.

## Elements — promise *and* peril
The debate is whether sub-replacement fertility is a crisis, a freely made choice, or
a relief — and what, if anything, states owe the decision. The art must render the
**shape of the population as the open question** — an age structure whose form is not
yet settled — and never a verdict, neither "collapse is underway" nor "decline is
fine":
- **A three-tier age-structure stack** — the focal point: three plain horizontal bars
  stacked with small even gaps and centered on one shared vertical axis, the
  population-pyramid form. All three bars **exactly equal in width**, so the stack is
  neither a widening pyramid nor an inverted top-heavy one. Which way the tiers go —
  or whether they move at all — is the topic's question. No taper, no flare, no
  arrow, no axis labels, no tick marks.
- **A simple cradle with a small rounded bundle resting in it** — echoing the topic's
  Phosphor `baby` glyph: a plain rounded cradle body on a shallow curved rocker,
  standing on the ground line, with one small filled rounded form inside it. Birth
  present and intact — the cradle is **not empty**, not tipped, not rocking, no motion
  lines, and no adult figures attend it (no family scene, no parent, no household).
- **A small simple globe with one meridian and one equator line** — the worldwide
  scope: sub-replacement fertility is spreading across very different societies, not
  one country's story. Whole and plain; no continents drawn, no crack, no arrows
  around it.
- **A single straight horizontal ground line across the scene** — one plane under the
  stack, the cradle, and the globe alike (mirrors the sibling compositions):
  demography and the individual decision sit on the same ground.

## Mood
Neutral and editorial — an age structure held at an even, undetermined shape, a cradle
with a child in it, a globe above. Whether the shape is a problem, whose choice it is,
and what a state may do about it are the open questions the scene holds. Not a
depopulation-doom scene, not a celebration of either fewer or more births.

## Avoid
Photorealism; more than one color; gradients/shadows; a stack drawn as a true
pyramid or as an inverted top-heavy shape, and any arrow, chevron, motion trail, or
dashed trajectory on it (verdict: which way the shape is going); a downward-sloping
line chart, a falling graph, a plummeting curve, a red-zone dial, or a rising
hockey-stick (verdict: crisis or boom); an empty cradle, a broken or tipped cradle, a
crossed-out or greyed-out child, a lone elderly figure among absences, empty swings,
a deserted playground, a shuttered school, a graveyard, or any decay/abandonment
motif (verdict: doom); a beaming crowded nursery, hearts, a growth sprout, or
confetti (verdict: pronatalist advocacy); a specific family configuration of any kind
— parents flanking a child, a couple, a pram pushed by a figure, wedding rings, a
house with a family inside — the scene must not comment on family form; national
flags, party symbols, government seals, medals, or cash incentives; clocks,
hourglasses, calendars, or countdowns; scale bars, tick marks, axis marks, percent
signs, or labels of any kind; fussy internal detail (faces on the bundle, blanket
texture on the cradle, continents or graticule mesh on the globe, hatching in the
bars); clutter; anything that reads as natalist advocacy or as antinatalist relief.

## Deliverable
Ideally **vector (SVG)**, single-color, transparent ground. PNG accepted
(the pipeline is raster-primary).

## Generation prompt (Nano Banana / Gemini)
**Frameless** — the art has NO drawn border. The CSS tile renders the rounded-square
frame. Lessons carried over from the earlier generations: force the soft fill to
~15–18% (it renders near-invisible if unstressed), force uniform stroke weight (thin
secondary elements drift thinner), and make every major element a **filled mass** —
earlier linework-only elements nearly vanished at 40px.

**Fresh-generation prompt:**
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration of a population-shape scene, in a clean editorial line-art style. Combine several simple symbols into one small, balanced composition: at the center, a simple three-tier age-structure stack — three plain horizontal bars stacked one above another with small even gaps, all three centered on the same vertical middle axis and all three EXACTLY the same width as each other, so the stack is a plain even column and NOT a tapering pyramid and NOT an inverted top-heavy shape, with no arrows, no axis, no tick marks and no labels; to one side, a simple cradle standing on the ground — a plain rounded cradle body on a shallow curved rocker — with one small rounded bundle resting inside it, so the cradle is occupied and not empty, sitting still with no motion lines and with no adult or parent figures anywhere; and above the other side, one small simple globe — a plain circle with a single curved meridian line and a single horizontal equator line, no continents. A single thin, straight horizontal ground line runs across the whole scene — the age-structure stack and the cradle both stand on it — no landscape, no hill.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene — just the illustration itself on a plain background. Keep the composition compact and centered, not spread wide, with the three bars of the stack identical in width and given equal emphasis — none wider, longer, darker, or more dominant than the others — and the small globe clearly smaller than the stack and the cradle.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element — the three bars, the cradle body and its rocker, the bundle, the globe's circle, meridian and equator, and the ground line must all be exactly the same thickness. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small, about 2.5 to 3.5 times the complexity of one simple glyph — each element stays simple, the richness is the combination. No fussy internal detail — no face on the bundle, no blanket folds or weave texture on the cradle, no continents or grid mesh on the globe, no hatching or shading inside the bars.
>
> Color: a single olive-green hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid olive (#7d8a4e), and give EVERY major element a clearly visible soft fill of the same olive at about 16-18% opacity — fill all three bars of the stack, the cradle body, the small bundle inside it, and the globe with a light olive tint so the two-tone duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors; no major element may be left as unfilled outline-only linework. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no offset or misregistered fills, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Composition: wide 3:2 aspect, centered with generous, even padding; balanced optical weight — the ground line runs level along the bottom, the even three-bar stack anchors the middle, the cradle sits on one side, the small globe above the other. Plain white background. Calm, neutral, editorial mood — a population's shape held even and undecided with the outcome left open; not a decline or depopulation scene, not a celebration of more births. No falling or rising line charts, no graph curves, no arrows, no dials or gauges, no clocks, hourglasses or calendars, no empty or broken cradle, no empty swings or playgrounds, no elderly figures, no couples, prams, families or houses, no hearts, no flags, no coins or money. No text, letters, words, numbers, tick marks, percent signs, or scale markings anywhere in the image.

**Edit-mode instruction (after the first generation, feed the concept back in):**
> Using this image, keep the same composition, elements, and layout, but: (1) remove any border or frame around the scene entirely; (2) add a clearly visible soft light-olive fill (~16% opacity of the same hue) inside EVERY major element — all three bars of the stack, the cradle body, the bundle inside it, and the globe — so it reads as a two-tone duotone with no outline-only elements left; (3) make every line exactly the same thickness, matching the main outline weight — including the globe's meridian and equator, the cradle's rocker, and the ground line; (4) make the three stacked bars exactly equal in width and centered on one shared vertical axis, so the stack is neither a tapering pyramid nor an inverted top-heavy shape, and remove any arrow, chevron, tick mark, axis or label near it; (5) keep the bundle resting inside the cradle so the cradle is occupied and upright, and keep the scene free of any adult, parent, couple, or family figures. Keep it flat, single olive hue, no gradients, shadows, extra colors, or text.

**Notes:**
- Over-generate on the **equal-width stack** and the **bundle in the cradle**; those
  are the elements most likely to come out wrong — the generator strongly wants to
  taper the bars into a real population pyramid (or invert them into a top-heavy
  wedge), to hang an axis, tick marks, or a downward arrow off the stack, and to
  leave the cradle empty or drop the bundle entirely.
- Watch for the generator editorializing — a falling graph line, a downward arrow, an
  empty or tipped cradle, a lone elderly figure, empty swings, or a parent-and-child
  pairing sneak in easily on this topic; any of those breaks the required neutrality
  and disqualifies the candidate. The mirror failure also disqualifies: hearts, a
  sprouting plant, or a crowded nursery reading as pronatalist cheer.
- Watch for family-form commentary — any adult figure attending the cradle, a couple,
  a pram, or a house-with-family reads as a statement about who should have children;
  the cradle must stand alone.
- Watch for outline-only elements — every major mass must carry the olive fill; a
  candidate whose bars or cradle render as bare linework fails the 40px test.
- White background is intentional — the primary sits on a neutral tile; the frame
  comes from the tile, not the art.
- After generating: save as `static/img/topics/birth-rate-decline-pronatalism.png`,
  set `Topic.primary_art = "img/topics/birth-rate-decline-pronatalism.png"`, and
  regenerate the social PNGs
  (`render_social --slug birth-rate-decline-pronatalism --formats og,sq,pt`).

## Selection

**Winner: `gemini-3-pro-image.4.jpg`** (2026-08-13, first generation round — no edit
passes needed). All four candidates delivered the cradle-stack-globe scene; scoring
against the brief:

- **#1** — four tiers instead of three, unequal widths, and a duplicated offset fill
  behind the globe (reads as a drop shadow — style violation). Fail.
- **#2** — equal-width three-tier stack, clean globe; but the cradle bundle rendered
  as a concentric double ring that reads as a target/camera at small size. Runner-up.
- **#3** — three tiers but tapered wider toward the base: exactly the pyramid read the
  brief forbids as its central neutrality device. Fail.
- **#4** — equal-width three-tier stack centered on one axis, occupied cradle with a
  soft organic bundle, clean globe (meridian + equator only), single ground line, all
  masses filled. Closest to the brief on every axis. **Winner.**

Known accepted deviation: fills render nearer ~35% opacity than the specced 16–18%
(consistent with every prior topic's accepted winners; post-processing normalizes
against the white ground).
