## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a raised outdoor rally stage with a lectern and a single microphone as the dominant focal mass, with a loudspeaker on a tall pole at one side; a small classical courthouse with a triangular pediment and four columns, no dome, sits on a rise behind the stage at a smaller scale, the stage's corner overlapping the base of the rise; a small ballot box at the smallest scale in the foreground; the ground is a broad rolling curve; a string of plain triangular bunting runs across the whole frame from the loudspeaker pole to the courthouse as the connective element.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #6a62b0, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: flags, eagles, domes, crowns, thrones, fists, chains, padlocks, cracks, crumbling or broken columns, ruins, fire, tanks, soldiers, handcuffs, megaphones pointed at the viewer, protest signs, crowds, scales of justice, gavels, scrolls, money, clouds or storms, arrows pointing down.
