## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: one compact coastal scene grouped in the center with wide white margins, nothing touching the edges: a tall slim ballistic missile standing upright in an open round silo on the crest of a low rounded hill on the left, the dominant focal mass, filled; the round silo lid lying open beside the silo, filled with the same light tint as everything else; a medium ballistic missile submarine riding low in the sea at the right, its sail and long hull filled, its bow overlapping the foot of the hill; a small long-winged bomber aircraft flying just above and right of the missile nose, filled; one single continuous ground line crosses the whole scene, rising over the hill and running down into a gently wavy sea surface under the submarine, as the connective element; the hill is filled only down to a soft gentle curve just below the ground line, never a flat bottom edge.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b3404f, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: mushroom clouds, explosions, fire, smoke, radiation trefoil symbols, skulls, doves, peace signs, broken or crossed-out missiles, flags, national markings, warning signs, countdown clocks, red buttons, globes with targets, crosshairs, people, faces.
