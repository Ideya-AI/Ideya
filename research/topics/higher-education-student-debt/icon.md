## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: one compact study scene grouped in the center with wide white margins, nothing touching the edges: a large graduation mortarboard cap with a hanging tassel resting on top of a tall stack of four thick books as the dominant focal mass, filled; a medium rolled diploma tied with a ribbon leaning against the book stack, overlapping it, filled; a small short stack of coins beside the books; a small classic columned university hall far behind on the horizon, at the end of a winding path that runs from the books back across the scene to the hall, as the connective element; the ground is a gentle rounded hill.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #9459a2, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: dollar signs, banknotes, money bags, chains, shackles, balls and chains, weights, anchors, padlocks, price tags, receipts, charts, graphs, arrows, ladders, cracks, falling or crumbling buildings, scissors, protest signs, flags, students, graduates, crowds, diplomas with writing, clocks, hourglasses, balance scales, question marks, bicycles, swings.
