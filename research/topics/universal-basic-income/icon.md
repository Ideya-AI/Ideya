# Universal Basic Income — topic art brief

The topic's primary art: `static/img/topics/universal-basic-income.png`, 1035x690 on white, shown at hero
size on the topic page, composed into the share cards and the poster, and reduced to a 40px tile.
Style, composition rules and the common negatives are supplied by `manage.py generate_icon`; this file
carries only the scene and this subject's own negatives, plus the selection log.

## Scene
a large mailbox on a post as the dominant focal mass, its door open and a plain envelope emerging;
three identical coin discs, unmarked, stacked and leaning at the foot of the post and overlapping
it; a curved row of small identical houses on the rise behind; a small wall calendar with a plain
grid at the right, resting on the rise; one path curving unbroken across the whole frame from the
lower left, behind the post, up past the houses as the connective element; the ground is the gentle
curve of a low rise.

## Negatives
dollar signs, currency symbols, banknotes, wallets stuffed with bills, piggy banks, hands, open
palms, scales of justice, charity ribbons, shopping carts, arrows going up or down.

## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a large mailbox on a post as the dominant focal mass, its door open and a plain envelope emerging; three identical coin discs, unmarked, stacked and leaning at the foot of the post and overlapping it; a curved row of small identical houses on the rise behind; a small wall calendar with a plain grid at the right, resting on the rise; one path curving unbroken across the whole frame from the lower left, behind the post, up past the houses as the connective element; the ground is the gentle curve of a low rise.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #b48f2c, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: dollar signs, currency symbols, banknotes, wallets stuffed with bills, piggy banks, hands, open palms, scales of justice, charity ribbons, shopping carts, arrows going up or down.

## Selection
Round 1, a giant unmarked coin with envelope, houses, calendar and a path: both samples let the coin
swallow the frame and read as a blank plate at tile size; r1-a also used two fill tones.

Round 2, the mailbox scene, two samples. r2-a is the whole topic in one picture — a payment arriving at
every house, every month — with real depth; r2-b arched the houses over a rainbow hill.

Round 3, a calendar as the dominant mass: both samples turned the grid into fussy detail that dissolves
small, and the coins shrank to specks. The scene was the problem, so it was dropped.

Round 4, an edit of r2-a: one continuous path instead of three broken strands, the floating calendar set
down on the rise, fills on the calendar and envelope. Picked `r4-b`, which made those fixes and kept the
calendar small; r4-a grew the calendar into what reads as a building. Normalized to
`static/img/topics/universal-basic-income.png`; the 40px tile holds the mailbox silhouette.

Spend: $1.07 across eight images. Glyph: `hand-coins`, the most literal single glyph for unconditional
cash and unused elsewhere in Economy & Work.
