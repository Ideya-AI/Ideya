## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a large dome tent pitched on a city sidewalk as the dominant focal mass, its door flap tied open; behind it and overlapped by the tent, a medium apartment building with a simple window grid and a plain front door; a rolled sleeping bag and a backpack resting beside the tent as the small element; a slim streetlamp at the right; one curb line curving gently across the whole frame from lower left to right as the connective element; the ground is the soft curve of the sidewalk rising slightly toward the middle.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #9459a2, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: people, figures, faces, hands, shopping carts, trash, litter, needles, syringes, bottles, police, badges, fences, barriers, bulldozers, signs, cardboard signs with writing, dollar signs, coins, cups, crosses, hearts, a row of identical tents, rain clouds, broken windows.
