## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: one compact neighborhood scene grouped in the center with wide white margins, nothing touching the edges: a tall apartment building with a simple grid of windows as the dominant focal mass, filled, a few of its windows lit with a slightly stronger fill and the rest plain; a medium row of small mailboxes on a post standing in front of the building, overlapping its lower corner, filled; a small single armchair glimpsed through one ground-floor window; a small rounded tree beside the building, partly hidden by it; one long telephone wire strung across the whole scene from the building to a pole on the far side, as the connective element; the ground is a gentle rounded hill.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #9459a2, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: human figures, silhouettes, crowds, faces, hands, hearts, tears, rain clouds, broken hearts, chains, cages, empty chairs at a set table, gravestones, phones with chat bubbles, robots, speech bubbles, charts, graphs, arrows, question marks, balance scales, park benches, street lamps, bicycles, kites, clotheslines, abandoned or boarded buildings, cracks.
