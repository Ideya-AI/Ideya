## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a large tall stack of plain paper application forms with blank ruled lines as the dominant focal mass, outlined and softly filled, slightly fanned so the edges of each sheet show; out of the top of the stack grows a simple decision tree of round nodes joined by smooth lines, overlapping the stack's upper corner, that branches downward and across the frame; each of its three end branches touches one small destination standing on the ground: a small house, a small briefcase and a small columned courthouse; everything sits on one broad gently rounded mound, outlined and softly filled; everything flat, no drop shadow.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #2f8f86, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: people, silhouettes, faces, eyes, robots, brains, scales of justice, gavels, check marks, crosses, thumbs, red or green lights, stop signs, warning triangles, police badges, handcuffs, police cars, sirens, dollar signs, coins, money, text, letters, numbers, logos, smartphones, security cameras, padlocks, servers, magnifying glasses.
