## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: A tall plastic water bottle with a screw cap stands as the dominant focal mass on a gently curved rise of ground. Behind it on the left, partly overlapped by the bottle, a small factory with two short stacks and a row of plastic pellets in a hopper. On the right, a lidded collection bin with a few containers beside it. A conveyor belt crosses the frame from the factory past the bottle's foot to the bin, carrying small bottles and tubs. A small plastic bag drifts above the bin.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #7d8a4e, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: recycling arrows symbol, sea turtle, dead animals, fish tangled in plastic, garbage heaps or overflowing landfill, smoke plumes, skull or warning signs, red circle-slash, crossed-out bottle, ocean gyre, logos, labels with text, people, hands
