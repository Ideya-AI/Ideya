## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: A large judge's gavel resting on its round sound block is the dominant mass in the foreground on a broad gently curved hill whose whole area under the curve is filled with a pale tint; behind it, partly hidden by the gavel head, a low prison building with a long wall, small barred windows and one watchtower sits on the hill; a path leaves the prison gate and winds across the hill to two small houses on the far right.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #6a62b0, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: handcuffs, chains, nooses, electric chair, gallows, syringes, blood, prisoners in stripes, silhouettes or figures, faces, broken bars, open cage doors, padlocks, tilted scales, blindfolded Lady Justice figure, courthouse dome or capitol, police cars, text, letters, numbers, frame, border, second color, shadows, gradients
