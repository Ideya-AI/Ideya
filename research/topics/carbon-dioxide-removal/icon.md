## Prompt
> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: a direct air capture unit as the dominant focal mass, a long low box whose face is a grid of large round fans; a pipe runs from its base down into a cut-away section of the ground, where it bends and descends through three wavy layers of rock strata to a small rounded pocket deep below; two round-crowned trees of medium size stand on the hill behind the unit, one overlapping its end; a few soft curved airflow lines drift across the whole sky toward the fans as the connective element; the ground is the gentle curve of a hill whose cut-away front shows the layers.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #3f9a6a, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: smokestacks, smoke, factories, oil derricks, flames, arrows, CO2 lettering or chemical formulas, globes, thermometers, melting ice, money, dollar signs, price tags, warning signs, protest signs.
