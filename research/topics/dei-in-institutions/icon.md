## Prompt

> A minimalist flat vector-style icon illustration, in a clean editorial line-art style: A broad glass-fronted office building with a front entrance stands as the dominant focal mass, set on one continuous gently curved hill with a soft fill. In front of it and overlapping its lower facade, a training session is set up: a flip-chart easel with a blank sheet, and two rows of plain stacking chairs angled toward it. A lanyard name badge hangs over the back of one chair. A small columned school hall sits on the far shoulder of the hill, and one unbroken winding footpath runs across the frame from the office entrance to the hall.
>
> Composition: one dominant focal mass with smaller supporting elements at clearly different scales (big, medium, small); at least one element overlaps another so the scene has depth; the ground is a gentle curve, never a flat line; one connective element runs across the frame to tie the pieces together. A single coherent scene, not a row of separate objects. Centered with generous, even padding.
>
> Style: monoline line art with ONE uniform stroke weight on every element, including the thinnest wisps and lines. Rounded line caps and corners. Confident, slightly bold strokes so it stays legible when small. No fussy internal detail.
>
> Color: a single hue, two tones only. Draw all lines in solid #9459a2, and give every major mass a clearly visible soft fill of the same hue at about 15-18% opacity, so the duotone is obviously present rather than white interiors. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture.
>
> Important: NO border, NO frame, NO box or outline around the scene, just the illustration on a plain white background. No text, letters, or numbers. No human figures or faces. Calm, neutral, editorial mood: show the subject plainly, neither celebrating nor warning. Also avoid: people, faces, hands, silhouettes, handshake, raised fist, rainbow, puzzle pieces, multicolored anything, scales of justice, protest signs, crossed-out or prohibition symbols, ladders, staircases, gates or barriers, open or closed doors as metaphor, letters or words on the chart, the letters DEI, logos, mirrored or balanced halves, ground drawn as a flat line, a frame or border
